# Clean After Use — the thirty, two weeks on

Second run, after the progression fix. Same thirty personas, same two weeks, same code.
Each review is grounded in what actually happened to that persona in the simulation —
sessions completed, weights moved, warnings they saw, bugs they hit.

**Average: 3.7 / 5.** Continuing: 24 of 30. Would recommend: 21 of 30.
Says it makes them train more: 22 of 30.

---

## 1. Kayla, 34 — complete beginner, machines, 3 days ★★★★★
6 of 6 sessions. Goblet squat 20 → 26.5 kg.

> I have walked into a gym before and walked straight back out. The difference here is the little
> drawings. I knew what a leg press was meant to look like before I sat on one. It told me what

## What the reviews add up to

**The fix worked.** Not one persona reported a runaway weight. Reece (18) explicitly noticed the
jumps now scale sensibly. Steph (11) had ten lifts hold weight while gaining reps and the app
correctly framed that as progress rather than a stall. Ian (17) and Gordon (15) both got the
stall flag and the deload button.

**The three five-star reviews all cite the same thing:** the app made a decision so they didn't
have to. Kayla (1), Marcus (7) and Sarah (28) each praised it for removing a reason not to go.

**The two-star reviews are both known bugs, not opinions.** Nathan (21) on the fortnight lookup
and Brandon (39) on unit conversion. Both said they'd return if fixed. Those are the next two
tickets and they are worth four rating points between them.

**The three-star reviews cluster into one complaint:** the app is built for someone earlier in
their training than they are. Dan (5), Alex (10), Sam (14) and Michael (30) all want per-set
logging, editable progression rules and an export. Three of the four are features that existed
before the rewrite.

**The most repeated request across all thirty** was supersets (Nicola 12, Amy 25) and pin numbers
for machine users (Peter 22, Gordon 15, Bill 3) — a third of the trial never touches a barbell
and gets no loading help at all.
