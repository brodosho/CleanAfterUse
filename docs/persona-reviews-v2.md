## The one holdout

**Alex (10)** is the only persona who won't use it as his program, and his reason is structural
rather than fixable by a setting: he wants percentage-of-max programming, planned blocks and a
peak. That is a different category of app. He keeps using it as a logbook, which is honest but
isn't what "continuing" should mean.

Getting him would mean building block periodisation — a large piece of work aimed at the smallest
slice of the thirty. Everyone else's blocker turned out to be a bug or a missing option.

## What still isn't solved

- **Thirty-minute sessions still hold two or three exercises.** Supersets help when there are
  accessories to pair; at thirty minutes the compounds use the whole budget. Nicola and Amy both
  accept it now because the app explains the levers, but it isn't fixed.
- **Kwame's two-a-days** drift from the schedule. Nothing breaks, but the plan and reality diverge.
- **Callum has to remember** to flip the easy-week switch. Nothing notices an in-season athlete.
- **22 of 30 still finish below the weekly set target** for at least one muscle. Honest arithmetic,
  but the warning still lives mostly at creation time.
